NEONVERSE

A piano studio, a 4-lane rhythm arcade, a synth workstation and a performance analyst in one place. Click anywhere to play a note.

Shortcuts: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 to enter, Esc to return home.

Octave 3

Play with Z–M and Q–I rows, mouse or touch. Hold a key to sustain, drag across keys to glide, Space holds the pedal, ↑↓ shift octave. Scale lock snaps wrong notes into the key of C.

Rhythm Arcade

Keys 1 2 3 4 (or tap the lanes). Tap notes, hold the long bars until they end, and on pink slide notes drag your finger or mouse across lanes. Esc quits.
